AMS:NOSA is the first integrated digital intelligence platform purpose-built for Africa's nuclear energy programme. It combines real-time data, interactive simulations, AI-powered analysis, multi-stakeholder communication, and science education into sovereign digital infrastructure — built and owned in Africa, for Africa.

New Simulations Funded by RAEB Investment
SMR
SMR Thermal-Hydraulic Model
Interactive heat exchange simulation for small modular reactors. Coolant flow, fuel loading, and power output are adjustable in real time to visualise temperature gradients and efficiency curves.
SCIENTIFIC VALUE · Calibrated to NuScale and Dual Fluid specifications. Usable for operator training scenarios.
DOSE
Radiation Dose Mapper
Visualises radiation dose in mSv across a plant perimeter, comparing nuclear boundary dose with natural background and medical exposure, replacing fear with data and transparency.
SCIENTIFIC VALUE · IAEA dose limit data integration. Public communication tool for RAEB outreach events.
DCY
Decay Chain Visualiser
Interactive Uranium-238 decay series through to Lead-206. Each isotope shows half-life, emission type, and real-world applications from power generation to medical imaging.
SCIENTIFIC VALUE · Supports RAEB's nuclear medicine and isotope application mandate.
GRID
Grid Stability Simulator
Rwanda's electricity grid with intermittent solar and hydro overlaid against baseload nuclear. Users simulate drought seasons, peak demand events, and industrial growth to understand baseload necessity.
SCIENTIFIC VALUE · Directly linked to Rwanda's 650 MW current capacity and 60 to 70 percent nuclear target.
FIS
Fission Yield Spectrum
Real-time mass yield distribution from U-235 thermal fission. The characteristic double-humped curve forms live as fission events accumulate, showing which isotopes are produced at what rates.
SCIENTIFIC VALUE · Bridges isotope production with waste management understanding and medical isotope supply chains.
PLS
Plasma Dynamics Engine
Visualises high-temperature plasma confinement principles relevant to fusion research, showing magnetic field lines, particle trajectories, and confinement stability in an animated field geometry.
SCIENTIFIC VALUE · Extends the platform from fission to fusion, positioning AMS:NOSA for next-generation nuclear science communication.
